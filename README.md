# Toronto & GTA Renovation Planner

**Costs, quotes, permits and rebates for your home renovation in Toronto, the GTA and Ontario.**
Plan a basement, legal basement apartment, kitchen, bathroom, home addition, full-home renovation or underpinning with realistic budget ranges, fewer surprises and better questions for your contractor.

## Try asking

- "How much does it cost to finish an 800 sq ft basement in Mississauga with a bathroom?"
- "Can I redo my kitchen for $30,000?"
- "Can my basement be a legal apartment in Brampton? The ceiling is 6'4"."
- "Are there rebates for a backwater valve and sump pump in Toronto?"
- "Compare these three quotes for my bathroom." *(paste or attach them)*
- "Do I need a permit to finish my basement in Vaughan, and how long will it take?"
- "Give me ideas for a basement gym and guest room, and what each would cost."
- "Write a project brief I can send to three contractors."
- "How do I check a contractor's licence and WSIB in Toronto?"
- "We want to renovate. Where do we start?"

## Sources and accuracy

- **Cost ranges** are market ranges from published 2026 guides (HomeStars Toronto price guides, RenovationFind, and the publisher's own market guides), each shown with its source and date. They're planning numbers, not quotes and not any company's price list.
- **Rules and programs** come from official pages (ontario.ca, canada.ca/CRA, toronto.ca, WSIB, Save on Energy), checked September 29–30, 2026. Programs change, so the plugin shows the date checked and the official link. When web search is available, it re-checks those pages first.
- This is general information, not legal, tax, engineering or financial advice. Cities decide permits; programs decide eligibility.
