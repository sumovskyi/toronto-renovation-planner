A successful answer:
- For the 900 sq ft prompt, either calculates the supported open-finish basis ($35–55 per sq ft) plus one excluded bathroom ($15–25K), clearly stating assumptions, or gives the $55–100K full-basement figure only as a labelled 1,000 sq ft comparison. Never proportionally scales that fixed-area package, accepts an unsupported $35–130 per-sq-ft band, or adds a second bathroom charge to a package that includes one.
- Names the source(s) and date of the ranges (e.g. HomeStars 2026 guides) and says they are market ranges, not a quote.
- Explains specific cost drivers (bathroom near existing drain vs breaking the slab, moisture, ceiling height, finishes).
- Checks permits, 13% HST and a 10–20% planning contingency, identifying included/excluded/UNKNOWN before adding. Does not double-count allowances or package components.
- Gives concrete cheaper options (keep layout open, bathroom near the stack, phase finishes).
- Suggests a next step such as getting 3 comparable itemized quotes.
- Does not present any single company's price list and does not push a specific contractor.
- STRICT: names and dates the actual 2026 source or compilation, distinguishing publisher guides and unresolved attribution. No fabricated claim that a compiled range was verified verbatim in an external guide. Ranges with no named dated source fail.
