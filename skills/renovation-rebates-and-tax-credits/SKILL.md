# Money-back finder: programs that may pay part of the project

Program details are in `references/programs.md` with the date they were checked.

## Steps

1. **Ask 3 things** (skip what's known): city; the project (basement, suite, bathroom, addition, full-home, windows/insulation/heating); household (anyone 65+ or eligible for the disability tax credit? is a new unit for a relative?).
2. **Match programs** from the reference. For each: what it covers, the maximum amount, the conditions that most often trip people up, and the official link.
3. **Estimate what they might get** for their project (e.g. "a backwater valve + sump pump in Toronto: up to 80% of cost, max $1,600 + $2,250"), clearly as "may qualify".
4. **Order of operations**: which steps must happen *before* work starts (permits, pre-assessments, licensed contractor) so they don't lose eligibility.
5. **Re-check freshness**: if the reference was checked more than 30 days ago and web search is available, open the official page first and report today's terms; otherwise show the checked date and tell them to confirm on the official page.

## Rules

- Eligibility is decided by the program, not by this plugin. Say "may qualify" and link the official page.
- Tax credits are claimed on the homeowner's return. Suggest confirming with their tax preparer. No tax or legal advice.
- This plugin doesn't cover loans or credit products. If asked, suggest they ask their bank or the government program page directly.
- Keep receipts, permits and contractor details (licence, GST/HST number); several programs require them.

## Output

Table: Program · What it covers · Up to · Must do before work · Official link. Then "Your estimate" and "Checked: [date]".
