# Programs (checked 2026-09-29; re-check before quoting if older than 30 days)

## City of Toronto: Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy Program
https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/managing-rain-melted-snow/basement-flooding/basement-flooding-protection-subsidy-program/
- Who: owners of single-family, duplex, triplex or fourplex residential properties in Toronto.
- Covers up to 80% of costs, max **$6,650 per property** (work completed on/after Nov 12, 2025):
  home plumbing assessment $500 (one-time) · backwater valve $1,600 per device, max two · sump pump + alarm $2,250 (one) · battery backup $300 · pipe severance and capping $400.
- Must: get a building permit for the backwater valve; check the contractor holds the City licence eligible for the specific work before it starts: the City table allows T94/T92/T87 for the listed work, while T85 Building Renovator covers sump-pump/pipe work but not backwater-valve installation or the plumbing assessment; verify the current table and licence; downspouts disconnected from the City sewer; no outstanding City taxes/debts; lifetime maximum per device type.
- Apply within two years of installation.
- Fits: basements, legal suites, underpinning, any project that opens the basement floor.

## Federal: Multigenerational Home Renovation Tax Credit (line 45355)
https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-45355-mhrtc.html
- Renovation expenses to create a self-contained secondary unit so a senior or an adult eligible for the disability tax credit can live with a qualifying relative.
- 14.5% of up to $50,000 per renovation, up to **$7,250** per claim (2025 tax year; the rate follows the lowest federal bracket, confirm the current year).
- Fits: basement suites, additions, garden/laneway suites for family.

## Federal: Home Accessibility Tax Credit (line 31285)
https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-31285-home-accessibility-expenses.html
- Qualifying individual: 65+ or eligible for the disability tax credit (or an eligible family member claiming).
- Up to **$20,000** of eligible expenses per year, credited at the lowest federal rate.
- Work must be permanent and improve access or mobility or reduce the risk of injury (e.g. walk-in shower, grab bars, ramps, wider doors). Not routine repairs, appliances, or work mainly for value.
- Keep receipts showing vendor, GST/HST number, work and dates.

## Ontario: Home Renovation Savings Program (Enbridge Gas + Save on Energy)
https://www.saveonenergy.ca/homerenovationsavings
- Bundle path (home energy assessment + 2 or more upgrades): assessment $600; insulation up to $7,700; windows/doors $100 per rough opening; air sealing up to $250; heat-pump water heater $500.
- Single upgrades (no assessment): cold-climate air-source heat pump up to $7,500; ground-source heat pump up to $12,000; attic insulation $1,250; smart thermostat $100; solar + battery up to $10,000; appliances up to $200.
- Deadline: UNKNOWN until checked on the current official program page. Do not quote a secondary-source closing date or assume funds remain available.
- Must: for the bundle path, book the pre-renovation energy assessment before starting work.
- Fits: full-home, additions, window replacement, basement insulation.

## Not covered by this plugin
