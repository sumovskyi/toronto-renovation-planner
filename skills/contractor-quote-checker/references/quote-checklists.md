# Quote checklists by project (Ontario)

Use as rows in the comparison. "Allowance" = a budget placeholder for items the homeowner picks later.

## Ontario rules (source: ontario.ca "Your rights when starting home renovations or repairs", read 2026-09-29)
- Contracts over $50 must be in writing and include: contractor name/address/contact; detailed description of work and materials; warranties; total cost and payment terms; schedule with start and completion dates; payment schedule with deposit; cleanup responsibility; sub-trades involved.
- 10-calendar-day cooling-off period if a contract of $50+ is signed in the home.
- Final price can't be more than 10% over the estimate unless the homeowner agreed to new work or a new price.
- Deposits: the Province recommends no more than 10%. Avoid cash deals.
- https://www.ontario.ca/page/your-rights-when-starting-home-renovations-or-repairs

## Every project
Permits and inspections (who applies, whose name) · drawings/engineering · protection of the house · disposal/bin · daily cleanup and final clean · HST shown · payment schedule tied to milestones · schedule with start and finish · change-order process (written price before extra work) · what happens with hidden conditions (water, mould, old wiring, asbestos) · warranty terms in writing (coverage, length, how to claim) · WSIB clearance and liability insurance · named project manager.

## Basement
Moisture check/fix · framing · insulation (type, R-value) · vapour control · electrical (panel capacity, lights, outlets) · HVAC supply/returns · plumbing rough-in and fixtures · drywall and ceiling type · flooring (allowance) · doors/trim · paint · stairs · egress window for bedrooms · sump/backwater valve · smoke/CO alarms.

## Legal second unit (add to basement)
Permit drawings and application · fire separation (ceiling/walls/doors, rated assemblies) · interconnected smoke + CO alarms · egress windows · separate entrance · HVAC separation or smoke dampers · electrical service capacity · sound separation · city registration where required · inspections to final.

## Kitchen
Design/3D · demolition · wall removal + structural (header, engineer) · plumbing and gas moves · electrical (dedicated circuits, lighting) · range hood vented outside · cabinets (maker, box material, soft-close) · countertop (material, template, install, sink cut-outs) · backsplash · flooring · appliance install · paint · weeks without a working kitchen.

## Bathroom
Demolition · subfloor/wall repair · plumbing (valves, drain moves) · named waterproofing system · tile + setting materials (allowance per sq ft) · fixtures supplied vs installed · exhaust fan vented outside · electrical (GFCI, heated floor) · glass · paint · days without the bathroom.

## Addition
Survey · zoning review / variance (Committee of Adjustment) if needed · architectural + structural drawings · permits · excavation/foundation · framing · roof tie-in · windows/doors · insulation · mechanical/electrical/plumbing extension · interior + exterior finishes · landscaping restoration · schedule split into approvals and construction.

## Full-home
Everything above per room, plus: one accountable contact · sequence (structure → systems → finishes) · decision calendar (what the homeowner must choose, by when) · occupied-home or move-out plan · contingency line.

## Underpinning
Engineer's design and stamp · permit · neighbour notice/agreement for shared walls · excavation in sections · footings and new slab · waterproofing + weeping tile · sump pit/pump and backwater valve · disposal · inspections · whether finishing is included.

## Red flags
Cash-only or cash discount · deposit over ~10% · "no permit needed" for structural, plumbing, legal-unit or egress work · one-line quote with no scope · "allowances as required" · no schedule · no warranty terms · pressure to sign today.
