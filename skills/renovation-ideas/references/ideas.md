# Ideas, constraints and common mistakes (general guidance)

## Basement
Ideas by budget: open family room/office · family room + 3-piece bathroom · gym or home theatre (sound insulation, dedicated circuits) · wet bar or kitchenette (plumbing near the stack) · guest suite (bedroom + bath) · legal second unit (see legal-basement-suite-check) · walkout to the yard (big light gain, structural and permit work).
Check first: water/moisture signs and grading · ceiling height at beams and ducts · where the main drain and stack are · egress for any bedroom · electrical panel capacity · furnace/water-heater clearances.
Cheaper: keep it open, keep the bathroom near the stack, box ducts neatly instead of moving them, standard doors and trim, finish in phases (rough-ins now, finishes later).
Mistakes: finishing over a moisture problem · failure to verify the applicable bedroom egress/exit requirements · forgetting HVAC supply/returns · no sound insulation under a living room · a bathroom far from the drain without budgeting a pump · skipping permits.

## Kitchen
Ideas: same-footprint refresh (new doors or refacing, counters, backsplash, lighting) · full replacement in the same layout · open the wall to the dining room (structural: beam, engineer, permit) · island (clearances: ~42–48 in / 107–122 cm walkways) · pantry wall of tall cabinets.
Check first: load-bearing walls · gas and plumbing locations · electrical circuits · range hood venting outside · floor under cabinets.
Cheaper: keep the sink, range and fridge where they are · stock or semi-custom cabinets · standard countertop sizes · phase appliances.
Mistakes: poor work-zone clearances · not enough outlets or lighting · a hood that doesn't vent outside · picking finishes before the layout · no plan for weeks without a kitchen.

## Bathroom
Ideas: refresh (vanity, toilet, fixtures, paint) · tub-to-shower with a curbless or low-curb entry · double vanity · heated floor · niche and good lighting · accessibility (grab-bar blocking, wider door).
Check first: subfloor and joists · drain locations · exhaust fan vented outside · waterproofing behind tile · shut-off valves.
Cheaper: keep the toilet and drain locations · standard-size tub/shower bases · one feature tile wall instead of all walls.
Mistakes: no proper waterproofing membrane · no or weak exhaust fan · poor lighting at the mirror · skipping blocking for future grab bars · removing the only tub in the house without thinking about resale or young children.

## Addition
Ideas: rear one-storey addition (kitchen/family room) · second storey over the existing house · garage conversion · garden or laneway suite (Toronto) · in-law suite for multigenerational living.
Check first: zoning (setbacks, height, lot coverage) · foundation and structure · mechanical capacity · permit/variance path · survey.
Cheaper: simple rectangular footprint · roof tie-in kept simple · as-of-right size (avoids a variance).
Mistakes: designing before checking zoning · underestimating approval time · forgetting HVAC/electrical capacity · no plan for the existing roof and rooms during the work.

## Full-home
Ideas: finish-led refresh (floors, paint, trim, lighting) · kitchen + bathrooms + floors · layout change and systems (electrical, plumbing, HVAC) · structural and exterior.
Order: structure → systems → insulation → drywall → finishes. Decide the big items (layout, kitchen, bathrooms) before the small ones.
Mistakes: renovating room by room and opening the same wall twice · no contingency for an older house · living in the house without a dust/phasing plan.

## Underpinning
Options: underpinning (lower the whole floor, most height) · benching (keep the footings, lower the middle; cheaper, loses some floor area) · leave as is and design around the height.
Check first: engineer's assessment · neighbour's wall on semis/rows · sump and backwater plan · soil and water table.
Mistakes: no engineer · no neighbour agreement on shared walls · pricing the dig but not the finish.
