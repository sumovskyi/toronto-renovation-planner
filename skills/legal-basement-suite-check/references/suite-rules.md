# Ontario second-unit (basement suite) rules: checked 2026-09-29

## Existing-house second-unit screening: applicability first
Source: City of Oshawa, Ontario Building Code 2024 guide (revised February 11, 2026), checked September 30, 2026: https://www.oshawa.ca/media/hqmd3xjs/obc-guide-to-basement-secondary-suite.pdf
This guide concerns second units in existing buildings. It does not cover an accessory-building unit or a third unit. Building age and the proposed exit arrangement affect the applicable pathway. Ask for those details and confirm the current municipality's requirements; missing context means CHECK, not a whole-suite PASS.
- The guide's existing-house ceiling screening figures are 1.95 m generally and 1.85 m under beams/ducts. Compare measurements only after identifying the applicable pathway; dimensional compliance alone does not establish legality.
- Bedroom egress, where required: at least 0.35 m² clear opening and no dimension under 380 mm. A direct exterior door can affect whether this window requirement applies; the window-well clear space is at least 550 mm under this pathway. Do not label every bedroom as needing an identical window without checking the exit context.
- The separate escape-window pathway has different conditions: at least 0.38 m² clear opening, no dimension under 460 mm, and the interior opening no more than 900 mm above the floor OR fixed steps, among other requirements. Do not apply its 900 mm figure as a universal bedroom-egress sill limit. Identify the applicable pathway with the designer/city before recommending work.
- Fire safety needs a design review: the guide provides smoke-tight-barrier and rated-separation approaches. Do not prescribe a universal 30-minute assembly. Check interconnected smoke alarms, carbon-monoxide alarms and the applicable separation/exit design with the city.
Always distinguish an arithmetic measurement check from building-code applicability and permit approval. The city's plans examiner decides.

## Provincial context
- Ontario permits up to three residential units on most urban residential lots (main house + two additional units), subject to municipal zoning details. Verify the current wording on ontario.ca or the city's page before quoting it.

## City pages (link, don't paraphrase what you haven't read)
- Toronto: search toronto.ca for "second suites" / "multiplex" and "building permit — second suite". Toronto also permits garden and laneway suites and, since 2023, up to four units (multiplex) as-of-right; verify on toronto.ca.
- Mississauga: second units must be registered with the City (search "Mississauga second unit registration").
- Brampton: second units must be registered (search "Brampton two-unit dwelling registration").
- Vaughan: permit applications through the City's online portal (search "Vaughan secondary suite permit").
- Markham, Richmond Hill, Oakville, Burlington, Hamilton: search "[city] second unit" or "additional residential unit" on the city's site.
If web search is available, open the city page and quote it with the date; otherwise give the search phrase and say "check the city's current page".
